# BestT9keyboard
A smartphone keyboard: Russian and other languages on a 3 × 4 keypad with word prediction (T9), English and Korean on a full QWERTY keyboard. The layout is chosen separately for each language.

## Initial setup
Open BestT9keyboard from the app list and go through "Initial Setup": enable the keyboard in Android and make it the default one. Then, in any text field, switch to BestT9keyboard with the keyboard button at the bottom of the screen.

It is recommended to disable the system spell checker (Android Settings → General management → Language and input). Otherwise words may be underlined in red, and the system "Add word" pop-up adds words to another keyboard, not to BestT9.

## Languages and layout types
Settings → "Languages and types".
- **Language list.** Every enabled language shows its layout: "3 × 4 (T9)", "QWERTY" or, for Korean, "Cheonjiin 천지인 (3 × 4)". Tap a language to change it.
- **Manage input languages.** Enable or disable languages.
- **Language switching method.** "Swipe on the spacebar" or "Hold the mode key".
- **Number row on QWERTY.** Show a row of digits above the letters.

### Dictionaries
Dictionaries are loaded and deleted automatically: enabling a language loads its dictionary, disabling it deletes it. Your added words are kept.

The Russian, English and Korean dictionaries are inside the app, no internet is needed. The other dictionaries are downloaded:
- when you enable a language in the settings, the keyboard asks first and shows the size;
- when you switch to a language without a dictionary, it is downloaded automatically on Wi-Fi. On mobile data, the keyboard switches to ABC and shows "Tap here to download".

## 3 × 4 (T9) layout
- **Keys 2–9**: letters. In T9 mode press each key once and the keyboard finds the word.
- **Key 1**: punctuation and smileys.
- **Left column**: four common characters: ! ? , ~
- **Hold a letter key** to type its digit, **hold the spacebar** to type "0".
- **⌫**: delete. **⇧**: capital letters. **OK**: Enter.
- **"+"**: add a word to the dictionary. **Hold "+"** to edit the word just typed.
- **Mode key** (bottom left): T9 → ABC (letter by letter, like on a keypad phone) → 123 (digits). Hold it to change the language.
- **Bottom row**: comma, spacebar and period.

### Suggestions
Matching words are shown above the keys, the most likely one first. The keyboard remembers the words you choose and moves them up next time.

### Hyphenated words
The hyphen between letters is not typed: Russian "из-за" is 4-3-3-2. Common hyphenated words are already in the dictionary.

### Adding a new word
1. Press the mode key to switch to ABC.
2. Type the word letter by letter.
3. Press "+".
4. Switch back to T9.

## QWERTY layout
- **⇧ Shift**: once = one capital letter, twice = Caps Lock (⇪), three times = off.
- **!#1**: digits and symbols. **1/2 and 2/2** switch the symbol pages. **ABC / АБВ / 한글** go back to letters.
- **Russian on QWERTY**: hold "е" for "ё", hold "ь" for "ъ".
- **Korean (2-set, 두벌식)**: letters are combined into syllables automatically: ㅎ + ㅏ + ㄴ = 한. ⌫ deletes the last letter inside a syllable.
- Number-only fields (e.g. phone numbers) open the digits and symbols right away.

## Korean Cheonjiin (천지인)
The Korean keypad layout. Vowels are built from ㅣ, ㆍ, ㅡ (keys 1–3): ㅣ + ㆍ = ㅏ. Consonants are on keys 4–9 and 0. The spacebar is in the right column.

## Switching the language
Swipe the spacebar: right = next language, left = previous. The arrows on the spacebar are a reminder. You can also hold the mode key at the bottom left.

## Top row
- **⚙ on the left**: settings.
- **🎤 on the right**: voice input, using the speech recognition of your phone (Google or Samsung).
- **In the middle**: suggestions and the current mode.

## Added words
Settings → "Languages and types" → "Added words": import, export and delete your words.
